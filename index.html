Questions

What you may want to know first

Which time zones do you cover?

I start at about 9pm Cebu time, which is UTC+8. That is 1pm in London and 8am in New York, so I cover UK afternoons and US mornings.

1pm to 5pm in London is 9pm to 1am in Cebu.
8am to noon in New York is 9pm to 1am in Cebu.

These times shift by one hour when the UK and US change their clocks. In Australia and New Zealand my hours are your night, from about midnight in Sydney and 2am in Auckland, so replies are ready by your morning.

How does access work?

The free test needs no access at all. You add me as a user in your helpdesk or shop inbox once we start working together, and I get my own login. You choose what I can see and do, and you can remove me at any time. Please do not send me your own password.

How do I pay?

The price is US$8 an hour. You set a weekly hour limit and I stay inside it. You pay once a week, after the work is done, so you never pay in advance. We agree on the payment method before I start.

What if I am not happy?

Tell me what you want changed and I will change it. The free test lets you judge my replies before you spend anything. You only pay for hours already worked, and you can lower the weekly limit or stop.

Do you answer phone calls?

No. I work in writing only, which means email, chat and social messages.
